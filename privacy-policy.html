Privacy Policy

Fancy Tetris — Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fancy Tetris app ("the App") handles information. The App is developed by Stefanos Voikos, based in Greece.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers. The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard local storage (shared preferences):

  • High scores for each game mode
  • Selected language / locale preference
  • Audio (music) on/off preference
This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertising (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before ads are shown, where required by law (for example, under GDPR for users in the EEA/UK). Through AdMob, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and other technical data in order to serve and measure ads; you can learn more about Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The App does not use separate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional, one-time, non-consumable purchase, "Remove Ads", which disables interstitial ads (rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch, remain available even after purchase). Purchases are billed and processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; the App does not receive, transmit, or store your payment card details. Whether you own "Remove Ads" is stored locally on your device. Refunds are handled according to Google Play's or Apple's own policies.

4. Permissions

The App may request access to device audio playback in order to play background music. No microphone, camera, contacts, or location access is requested.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because no personal information is collected at all.

6. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's storage/data from your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page and in the App with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer, Stefanos Voikos, at: Stefanos Voikos — elstefanosvks7@gmail.com
